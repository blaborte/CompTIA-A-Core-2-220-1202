A+ Core 2 (220-1202) Exam Details

A breakdown of the official exam objectives and key information.

Exam Summary

Passing Score
700
Max Questions
90
Question Types
MCQ & PBQ

To pass, you must achieve a score of **700** on a scale of 100-900. The exam features a mix of multiple-choice questions (MCQs), drag-and-drop, and performance-based questions (PBQs) that test your hands-on skills.

Core Exam Domains

  • Operating Systems (28%)

    Covers installation, configuration, and management of **Windows, macOS, and Linux**. Includes knowledge of command-line tools, system utilities, and file systems.

  • Security (28%)

    Focuses on identifying and mitigating security threats. Topics include **malware removal, security protocols, encryption**, and best practices for securing networks and devices.

  • Software Troubleshooting (23%)

    Tests your ability to resolve common software issues on both PC and mobile devices, including problems with applications and operating systems.

  • Operational Procedures (21%)

    Covers best practices for IT professionals, such as **documentation, safety procedures, backup and recovery**, and professional communication.
